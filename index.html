About Neon Nitro

Neon Nitro is a street racing game with bright cartoon cars. Race through a glowing night city, over sunset highway hills and along a coast road, weaving through traffic as close as you dare for Near Miss combos.

The career has 18 events in three districts: sprints against rivals, near-miss score rushes, time attacks, patrol escapes and a boss duel at the end of each district. Win stars to open new districts and six cars, and spend coins on engine, nitro and handling upgrades, paint and underglow.

3 quick tips

  1. Press NITRO right as the lights say GO for a perfect start.
  2. Near misses in a row build a combo. Bumping traffic ends it.
  3. Hold DRIFT while steering through bends to fill nitro fast.

How to play

🚗 Your car speeds up on its own. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer (or tilt your phone, see Settings). On a keyboard use the arrow keys or A / D.

🔥 NITRO gives a big burst of speed. Fill it by passing traffic closely, drifting and riding in a rival's slipstream. Keyboard: Space.

💨 DRIFT: hold the DRIFT button while you steer to slide through bends. Let go to get a speed kick. On its own, the button brakes. Keyboard: Down or Shift.

⚡ Near Miss: pass cars as close as you dare. Quick near misses in a row build a combo for big points. Bumping into traffic slows you down and ends the combo.

🚨 Patrol Escape: patrol cars try to pull up beside you and box you in. Keep your speed up, weave through traffic so they get stuck, find the gap in roadblocks and reach the finish.

⭐ Win stars to open new districts and cars. Spend coins in the Garage on upgrades, paint and underglow.
